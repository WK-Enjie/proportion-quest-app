📖 How to Play

👥

Two Players

Player 1 (Student) vs Player 2 (Teacher or Friend). Take turns answering questions to earn points and defeat your opponent!

🗺️

5 Worlds · 3 Levels Each

Journey from basic proportion identification all the way to 3-variable challenges. Complete each world to unlock the next!

🎡

Spin the Wheel!

Before each question, spin the wheel to get your question type — MCQ, Table, Find k, True/False, Fill in the Blank, or Word Problem!

⚡

Power-Ups

⏰ Time Freeze — Get 15 extra seconds
💡 Hint — Eliminate 2 wrong answers
💣 Double Down — Double your points
🛡️ Shield — Block next heart loss

🎯

Scoring

✅ Correct Answer: +100 to +250 pts
⚡ Speed Bonus: up to +50 pts
🔥 Streak ×2: 2 correct in a row
🔥 Streak ×3: 3+ correct in a row
❌ Wrong Answer: Lose a heart ❤️
